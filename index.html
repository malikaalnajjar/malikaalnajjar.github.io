MicroMobility Platform

Booking, Check-in & POS Platform · PWA

The production platform behind MicroMobility's group rides and events in Jeddah. Riders book in 10 languages with Google or Apple sign-in and get Apple Wallet passes; staff run check-in (including NFC bike tags), point of sale, inventory, refunds and reports from their own phones, kept in sync in real time. PostgreSQL on Supabase with row-level security, deployed on Cloudflare, with a Playwright end-to-end suite in CI.

SupabasePostgreSQLJavaScriptPWACloudflare PagesPlaywrightGitHub Actions
Challenge
Replace paper queues at a real rental operation: bookings, waivers, check-in and payments on busy ride nights, across several staff phones at once, with customer data locked down.
Approach
One vanilla-JavaScript PWA for riders and staff on Supabase: Postgres with row-level security and staff-only functions, realtime updates instead of polling, and a service worker for install and offline resilience. Cloudflare Pages Functions sign the Apple Wallet passes, and every change passes lint, type and translation checks plus a Playwright suite that stubs the database, so tests never touch production.
Impact
In production every ride night: staff check riders in, assign bikes and take cash or card on their own phones, while riders manage their own bookings in 10 languages, including Arabic and Urdu right-to-left.

AUJ / أوج

Smart Retail Shelf System

Dual-sensor smart shelf for small retailers: a load cell and camera on ESP32-S3 publish events over MQTT to a local FastAPI + PostgreSQL hub (29 REST routes, live WebSocket). A fine-tuned YOLOv12n verifier cross-checks every weight event against its camera clip, LightGBM quantile models (q10/q50/q90) forecast per-SKU demand, and a five-rule engine reprices within a ±15% band with a full audit log. Zero PII, fully offline, 143 passing integration tests, 80 SAR per shelf.

ESP32-S3MQTTPostgreSQLFastAPILightGBMYOLOv12ByteTrackWebSocketReact
Challenge
Give a small Saudi retailer real-time shelf visibility: live inventory, customer behaviour, demand forecasts, and dynamic pricing, on commodity hardware, fully offline, with zero personally identifying data (PDPL-aligned) and an 80 SAR per-shelf budget.
Approach
Each shelf pairs a 10 Hz load cell running a four-state FSM with a camera: confirmed events publish over MQTT while an 8-frame clip uploads over HTTP. The local hub (FastAPI + PostgreSQL 16) cross-checks weight against a fine-tuned YOLOv12n verifier with a frame-diff fallback, tracks aisle behaviour with ByteTrack using transient IDs only, forecasts demand with LightGBM quantile models trained on the M5 dataset, and reprices daily through five rules in strict precedence inside a ±15% band.
Impact
Pick-to-dashboard latency in seconds, every price change audit-logged, sensor disagreements raised as discrepancy alerts, and 143 integration tests passing against a real PostgreSQL instance. All data stays in the store: no cloud, no PII.

GASTAT Finalist

Sadeed / سديد

Data Quality & Validation Platform

Real-time data quality guard for GASTAT field surveys: a browser extension that catches semantic contradictions (a 19-year-old "general manager", a salary with no job) as the record is typed, before submission. Three escalating layers: Great Expectations structural checks, a One-Class SVM statistical gatekeeper, and a local Arabic-capable LLM (Qwen 2.5 via Ollama) that explains each conflict. Detection lifted from 23% to 87.5% (F1 0.829 on real Labour Force Survey data) at over 97% precision, with zero data leaving the government network.

FastAPIRedisGreat ExpectationsOne-Class SVMQwen 2.5OllamaDockerBrowser Ext.
Challenge
Field surveys collect semantic contradictions rigid rule systems cannot see: a 19-year-old "general manager", a respondent with no job but a 12,000 SAR monthly income, work experience exceeding the respondent's age. Unreliable records reach the national data warehouse and are only discovered when re-collection is already expensive.
Approach
A browser extension validates each record live through three escalating layers: Great Expectations structural checks, a One-Class SVM gatekeeper that forwards only suspicious records at near-zero cost, and a swappable local LLM (Qwen 2.5 / Mistral 7B via Ollama, few-shot prompted, full Arabic support) that judges semantics and explains each conflict. Redis queues scale it to thousands of forms a day, and an open REST API integrates with any survey platform without modifying agency systems.
Impact
Anomaly detection rose from 23% with rules alone (F1 0.374) to 87.5% (F1 0.829 on real Labour Force Survey records) at over 97% precision with fewer than 5 false positives. Citizen data never leaves the government network. Qualified to the national GASTAT Hackathon finals.

Smart Adventure Tourism Platform

Adventure-tourism platform making Aseer's mountain trails safe and bookable: a Flutter + Mapbox app with trail booking, an AI + IoT early-warning system (AWS IoT Core, SageMaker) watching trail conditions, a network of accredited local guides, and a gamified "Aseer Peaks Passport" stamped per completed trail. FlutterMapboxNode.jsPostgreSQLFastAPIAWS SageMakerAWS IoT CoreStripe
Challenge

Approach
As AI developer on a five-member team: a Flutter + Mapbox app backed by Node.js/Express and PostgreSQL, with Python/FastAPI and scikit-learn models on AWS SageMaker. AWS IoT Core sensors feed the trail early-warning system; the "Peaks Guide" flow turns a certified guide's trail video + GPS into a draft route with rest, danger, and service points for expert review; QR entry manages trail access, with Stripe payments and OpenWeatherMap forecasts.
